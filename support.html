I’m stuck on a level. What can I do?

Use your boosters: Hint, Peek, Remove and Place. Coins you earn by winning levels buy more. If you’re out of moves, tap the 🏳️ flag at the top to retry the level or try the Gauntlet: fresh letters and one try to spell a single word of 5 or more letters.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved only on your device. There’s no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Restoring your new phone from a backup of your old one usually brings it along.

How do Friend Boards work?

On the trail map, tap “Make a Friend Board”, add 5–20 words (names, inside jokes, anything with 3–7 letters) and share the link. Your friend opens it in their web browser and plays a board where your words score double. Everything is inside the link, so only share it with people you’re happy to have read it.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Tap the music-note button at the top of the game screen.

Can I see the story again?

Tap the 🏳️ flag during a level and choose “Replay the story”.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. Tilespell Trail is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.
